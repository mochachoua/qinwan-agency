## 6. Customer problems

**[RECOMMENDATION]** — problems to address in copy:

1. **"We don't know where to start."** The UAE looks attractive but the entry path is unclear.
2. **"Is there a real market for us?"** No local data or validation of demand and competition.
3. **"Our home playbook may not work."** Messaging, pricing, channels and buying habits differ.
4. **"Setup is not a strategy."** Many providers sell a licence; few help sell the product.
5. **"We have no local network."** Lack of partners, distributors, resellers, introductions.
6. **"Acquisition is expensive and uncertain."** Testing channels in a new market wastes budget without a plan.
7. **"We can't afford a slow, costly mistake."** Expansion consumes time and leadership attention.

## 22. Logo directions

**[RECOMMENDATION]** — to explore, not final:

**A. Pure wordmark "QINWAN"**
- Uppercase, widely tracked, refined sans or high-contrast serif.
- Distinctive detail on the Q tail (e.g. a forward-moving stroke suggesting expansion).
- Most corporate, safest, strongest legibility.

**B. Monogram "Q"**
- Geometric Q built from a circle and a directional stroke/opening — "a market (circle) and an entry point (stroke)".
- Works as LinkedIn avatar, favicon, app icon, document seal.
- Paired with wordmark for full lockup.

**C. Abstract symbol — expansion / connection**
- Two shapes meeting or a line crossing a boundary (market entry); or an arc that opens outward.
- Can echo an 8-point geometric star in very reduced form — subtle regional reference.
- Risk: genericness; must stay extremely simple.

**D. Premium typographic / bilingual lockup — PRIMARY DIRECTION [FACT: logo built around قنوان]**
- The Arabic word **قنوان** is the heart of the logo, paired with the Latin wordmark QINWAN in a matching weight and proportion.
- Explore: the Arabic word as the symbol (calligraphic or geometric Kufi construction) above or beside QINWAN; letterforms whose dots/curves echo date seeds or a cluster.
- Must remain legible and refined for an English-speaking audience: Arabic as signature, Latin for reading.
- Strong, authentic regional signal without clichés.

**E. Date-inspired mark**
- Q whose tail or counter suggests a date seed/oval, or a minimal cluster of 3 ovals.
- Ties the logo to the name's meaning; must stay geometric and abstract (no fruit illustration, no palm tree).

Requirements for all: one-colour version, works at 16 px, horizontal + stacked lockups, mono/negative versions, no buildings, no skyline, no falcon/palm/dhow clichés, no gradients.

## 23. Typography direction

**[RECOMMENDATION]**
- **Headlines:** a refined serif or sharp grotesk with character — e.g. *Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display* (editorial, premium) or *Manrope / Söhne-style grotesk* (modern corporate). Choose one route.
- **Body:** a highly legible neutral sans — e.g. *IBM Plex Sans*, *DM Sans* or *Helvetica Neue*.
- **Arabic (logo only):** a refined Kufi or Naskh treatment for قنوان, custom-drawn or based on e.g. *IBM Plex Sans Arabic*, *Noto Kufi Arabic* or *Amiri* as a starting point.
- Rules: max 2 families; large confident headings; comfortable body (16–18 px web); generous line height; sentence case.
- Avoid: overused default stacks (Inter, Roboto, Arial), script fonts, faux-Arabic Latin fonts.
